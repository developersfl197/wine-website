Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Taylor Family Vineyards ships wine only to destinations where direct shipment is permitted and where we are authorized to do so. Available destinations and shipping methods may change as laws, carrier services, and winery permits change.

Orders are shipped by UPS or FedEx. Shipping charges include standard packaging and will be shown at checkout or communicated before an order is finalized. We will send a shipment notification to the email address associated with the order when tracking information becomes available.

Someone who is at least 21 years old must be available to receive and sign for every wine shipment. Carriers will not leave wine without the required adult signature. We recommend using a business address or another address where an adult is ordinarily available. The customer is responsible for additional charges resulting from an incorrect address, repeated delivery attempts, refusal, or failure to collect a shipment, except where prohibited by law.

Weather Holds and Delivery Timing

Wine is sensitive to temperature. To protect the condition of an order, we may delay shipment during periods of excessive heat, freezing temperatures, severe weather, or other conditions that may compromise the wine. Any shipping or release date we provide is an estimate and may change. We may contact you to discuss an expedited method or an alternate delivery window, but we will not ship when we reasonably believe conditions are unsafe for the wine.

Returns, Damage, and Delivery Issues

Because wine is a perishable product, all sales are final except where an order is damaged in transit, incorrect, flawed, or otherwise eligible for return under applicable law. Personal preference is not a basis for a return. Please inspect your shipment promptly and contact us at wine@taylorfamilyvineyards.com within seven days after delivery if bottles are missing, incorrect, damaged, leaking, or appear to have been compromised in transit. Include the order number and photographs when practical. We may ask that the affected wine and packaging be retained while we review the claim.

When a claim is approved, we may replace the wine, issue a credit, or refund the applicable purchase price and original shipping charge, depending on availability and the circumstances. We cannot accept responsibility for wine damaged after delivery through improper handling or storage.

Questions

For assistance with an order or shipment, contact wine@taylorfamilyvineyards.com.
