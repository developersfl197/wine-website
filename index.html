The Estate

This vineyard has always been
more than a place to grow grapes.

It began simply. In 1980, the Taylors planted ten acres of vineyard in what would become the Stags Leap District, guided by the belief that great wine begins with tender care for the land. Over time, the next generations stepped in, continuing the work with the same steady hand. In 2002, the Taylor Family Vineyards label was created and gave a name to what had already been quietly taking shape for decades.

As the vineyard matured, so did the understanding of it. Each year adding something subtle, something earned. Today, it is still exactly that, a family effort. Still rooted in the same place. And still evolving.

A family story,
still in motion.

Jerry Taylor was fifth generation Napa Valley, growing up in Monticello, a place that no longer exists, but remains part of the family story. He and Pat settled in Yountville in the 1950s, where they raised their family.

In 1976, they purchased this land. Then, just a hillside and a vision. Four years later, the family planted the first vines by hand. By the early 2000s, the vineyard was proving itself. The wines being made from its fruit were gaining recognition, and Napa Valley was entering a new era. In 2002, Sandy Taylor and her husband Phil Carlson established the winery, giving a name and a home to what the land had already begun to show.

Today, the estate remains entirely in family hands. Farmed, managed, and cared for by the same people who call it home. Three generations, one passion.
